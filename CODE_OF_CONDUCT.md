# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in androidMTP a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

- Be respectful and constructive in issues and PRs
- Assume good intent; disagree on technical grounds
- No personal attacks, bigotry, or doxxing

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer via GitHub issues (private contact if needed). Maintainers may warn, moderate, or ban contributors who violate these standards.
